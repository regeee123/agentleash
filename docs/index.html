AgentLeash · Policy Demo

An AI agent,
on a leash.

AgentLeash is a policy-guarded wallet for AI agents on NEAR. The agent proposes payments; a policy engine screens them, a vault contract enforces a hard cap, and the agent's key — scoped by the NEAR protocol itself — can do nothing else. Everything on this page is a recorded testnet outcome, not a mock-up.

DEMONSTRATION · recorded NEAR testnet proofs · no real funds
01

The rules the agent lives under

Set in policy.json and in the vault contract at vault.agentleash2026.testnet.

0.5 NEAR

Per-payment cap

Enforced twice: by the policy engine before signing, and again by the vault contract on chain.

1 NEAR

Daily cap

Total per day, tracked in the agent's own spend ledger. A proposal that would cross it is refused.

1 merchant

Allowlist only

Only shop.agentleash2026.testnet can be paid. Anything within edit distance 2 of it is treated as a scam lookalike.

0 deposit

Deposits forbidden

The agent's function-call key cannot attach a deposit — the NEAR protocol rejects it. Payments come out of a pre-funded vault float instead.

03

The deposit rule — a protocol finding

Why the money sits in a vault float at all.

The obvious design — the agent attaches a deposit to each pay call through its function-call key — is impossible on NEAR. We tried it on testnet: the chain rejected the call before any of our code ran, and the key's allowance was untouched.

AccessKeyDepositWithFunctionCallActionError — “Deposit is not allowed with function call”

So the owner funds a small float into the vault contract once, and the leashed key merely directs payments out of it — calling one method, on one contract, with a gas-only allowance. The discovery is recorded in evidence/deposit-restriction-discovery.json.
